After the haul

That net didn’t only catch fish.

Our catch is an illustration, but the problem behind it is real. Estimates suggest around

11million tonnes

of plastic enter the ocean every year, mostly from land. Without change, that could rise to as much as 37 million tonnes a year by 2040.

From street to sea

An estimated 2.7 million tonnes of that plastic travels down rivers. Litter dropped on a street could reach the sea through drains and waterways.

Mostly plastic

Plastic is estimated to make up at least 85% of marine litter. Light packaging can be carried off by wind or runoff, then it lasts for years.

Lost gear

Abandoned or lost fishing gear is thought to be about 10% of marine litter, up to a million tonnes a year. Loose line can entangle turtles, seabirds and mammals.

Global estimates from UNEP and WWF; figures vary by study. They describe the wider problem, not this illustrated net.
